Fish-Operated Vehicle

September 2026. Team of two. Designed, built, and demoed in under 24 hours.

A wheeled vehicle that goes wherever the fish riding on top of it swims.

A camera on a Raspberry Pi 5 watches a small tank mounted on the chassis. Computer vision picks the fish out of the water, estimates which way it is pointing and how hard it is swimming, and turns that into drive commands for four mecanum wheels, so the vehicle strafes, turns, and rolls wherever the fish decides to go. The demo video passed 40,000 views and 1,800 likes on Instagram in its first 72 hours.

How it works

  1. Pi 5 camera feed
  2. Dual HSV + LAB segmentation
  3. Moment-based heading and swim-effort estimate
  4. Holonomic drive commands
  5. Serial to Pico 2 W
  6. Mecanum-wheel controller

The hard part

Moving a tank of water is a feedback loop: accelerate, and the water sloshes; the slosh moves the fish; the fish's new position becomes the next command. We rate-limited how quickly the drive commands are allowed to change, with different limits for ramping up and ramping down, and tuned it until the tank stayed calm while the vehicle kept following the fish.

Diabetes Alert Service-Dog System

August to December 2025. Team of four. Most Innovative Design, Duke Engineering Design Expo, selected from 35+ teams.

A wireless alert triggered by a service dog's bite, delivered to the handler's wrist with no phone or Wi-Fi in the loop.

Our client has Type 1 diabetes and a diabetic-alert service dog. We built the dog a way to raise an alarm the handler can't miss: biting a 41-gram pressure-sensing bringsel makes a bracelet on the handler's wrist vibrate. Two XIAO ESP32-C3 boards talk directly over ESP-NOW, so there is no phone, no Wi-Fi, and no pairing to fail, and a charge lasts over six hours.

How it works

  1. Sustained bite on the bringsel
  2. Pressure sensor in a sewn biothane housing
  3. XIAO ESP32-C3 applies hold-time and cooldown logic
  4. ESP-NOW link
  5. XIAO ESP32-C3 in the bracelet
  6. Vibration on the handler's wrist

My role

Dog-side hardware lead. I integrated the pressure sensor into a sewn biothane housing, balancing bite resistance against consistent actuation, did most of the unit's soldering, and specified the trigger logic, a sustained-bite hold plus a cooldown, that keeps an idle chew from becoming a false alarm.

Poker Equity Calculator

August 2026 to present. Solo. Pure Python standard library.

A Texas Hold'em equity engine, built from scratch and checked against the published numbers.

Give it hole cards, opponents, and any part of the board, and it returns each hand's chance of winning. Heads-up matchups are enumerated exhaustively; multi-opponent spots run Monte Carlo. Underneath is a 7-card hand evaluator with full tiebreaker logic, covered by pytest cases for the awkward corners: wheel straights, kicker ties, full houses.

How it works

  1. Hole cards and board
  2. Remaining deck
  3. Exhaustive enumeration (heads-up) or Monte Carlo deals (multiway)
  4. 7-card evaluator with tiebreakers
  5. Win and tie tallies
  6. Equity per hand

The hard part

Trusting the answer. An evaluator that is wrong one time in a hundred still produces plausible-looking equities. So each edge case, wheel straights, kicker ties, full houses, has its own pytest case, and the engine is validated against published equities for 20 benchmark matchups, landing within ±0.5% on all of them.

In progress

An OpenCV card-recognition extension using Pillow-rendered card assets and template matching, so cards can be read from an image instead of typed in.
